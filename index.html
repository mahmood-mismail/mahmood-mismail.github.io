Dailymotion

Paris - Dubai / Remote · Feb 2020 — Feb 2024

Partnerships Manager — MENA

I joined Dailymotion in February 2020 with a clear mission: build and grow the company's MENA business. Historically Dailymotion operated primarily as a global video platform (still one of the largest with roughly 450M MAU generating 4B video playbacks). However, its strategy had evolved toward making its video technology available to publishers and enterprises. This created two distinct commercial models that I managed across MENA: Dailymotion Advertising and Dailymotion PRO.

DAILYMOTION ADVERTISING

Building a publisher monetization business

Dailymotion Advertising allowed publishers to deploy Dailymotion's video player technology across their web and native environments, allowing Dailymotion to monetize the publisher's video inventory programmatically through Dailymotion’s ADX in return for a rev-share. My responsibility was to build this ecosystem across MENA from the ground up.

I researched and targeted major publishers and broadcasters including CNBC Arabia, Dubai Media, Abu Dhabi Media, Al Arabiya, Asharq, beIN Sports, Mawdoo3, WebTeb, Layalina, 7elloha, Rotana and many more.

DAILYMOTION PRO

Enterprise SaaS and Video Technology

Alongside the advertising business, I also sold Dailymotion PRO — the same underlying video technology packaged very differently for enterprise customers. Unlike Dailymotion Advertising, PRO was a white-label, subscription-based technology platform against a fee paid monthly, quarterly or yearly based on consumption, usage and features. The platform was primarily suited to businesses that wanted to deliver video but did not want to monetize their inventory through advertising.

THE SALES PROCESS
Research → Prospect → Lead Generation → Discovery → Technical Validation → Negotiation → Integration → Monetization → Account Growth

My prospecting was heavily research-driven. I would study the prospect’s existing tech stack and video setup and, if needed, refer to tools such as BaitWith and Similarweb for accuracy before reaching out to the prospect. For strategic prospects, I would invest quality time preparing customized pitch decks showcasing what was already working and where opportunities existed via Dailymotion’s tech stack.

Selling Dailymotion was not simply about selling a video player. I was also managing the technical discovery, configuration, integration guidance, migration, testing, QA, go-live and post-launch optimization.
