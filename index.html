ABOUT

Connecting technology with commercial growth.

For the past 13 years, my career has largely been about taking complex products, platforms and business models and turning them into commercial opportunities.

In January 2013, I started my career at the Dataflow Group, building institutional relationships across government, healthcare and higher education organizations across MENA. 7 Years later in February 2020, I was appointed as Partnerships Manager - MENA at Dailymotion where I stepped into digital media and enterprise sales for the mission of building and growing Dailymotion’s MENA publisher ecosystem and B2B SaaS sales from the ground up. After 4 successful years at Dailymotion, in Feb 2024 I joined Khaleej Times and expanded my media journey towards the integrated media, working across Digital, Print, Social and Content while contributing to commercial strategy, client growth and sales operations.

Today, my strongest area sits at the intersection of Enterprise sales, Strategic Partnerships, Digital Media, AdTech, MediaTech, SaaS and Data. I enjoy understanding how a product actually works, finding where the commercial opportunity sits, and then building the relationships, proposition and execution needed to turn that opportunity into revenue.
